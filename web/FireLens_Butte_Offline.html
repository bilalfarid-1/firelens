04 / EVIDENCE & LIMITATIONS

Transparent by design.

Here is how we handle unlike instruments without inventing precision.

Validation CSV ↓
01

Comparable support

Presumed vegetation-fire detections (type 0) are assigned to fixed 1-km equal-area cells. Repeated pixels in one UTC day and cell count once per sensor.

02

Empirical crosswalk

MODIS monthly cell-days predict VIIRS-reference cell-days using a robust log-linear fit on 2013–2023. Year-block cross-validation selects the simpler fit.

03

True held-out years

Neither 2024 nor 2025 influenced model fitting or selection. Error is checked against VIIRS observations—not against a verified wildfire inventory.

2024–2025 UNTOUCHED TEST · 24 MONTHS

Measured agreement, not ground-truth accuracy.

Crosswalk MAE: 20.33 cell-days/month vs 32.00 for unadjusted MODIS grid counts. The model still badly misses July 2024: estimated 340.8 vs 58 VIIRS cell-days.

Correlation is not proof of accuracy. Sensor coverage is unverified.
Scientific and safety note. Hotspots are satellite-detected thermal anomalies. This is not a wildfire-count, burned-area, emissions, hazard, alerting, emergency-response, or real-time product. Cloud cover, overpass timing, thresholds, and spatial resolution affect detections. Early estimates are exploratory and may not generalize.
